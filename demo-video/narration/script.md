# English narration

## 01-hook — Science produces more than success.

Hello everyone. We are Laplace Flower Shop. A failed synthesis or fading battery can contain useful evidence, yet too often it stays inside one laboratory.

## 03-idea — SciExchange

SciExchange brings scientific screening, provenance, reciprocal exchange, and human consent into one workspace, helping useful evidence move while preserving its context.

## 04-workflow — From evidence to authorized exchange.

The workflow connects numerical checks, attestations, matching, and analysis. A React interface communicates with a FastAPI gateway, transaction store, and independent signature verification.

## 05-boundaries — Explicit checks. Explicit limits.

Inspired by machine checkable mathematics, our compiler performs numerical screening, not a completed Lean proof. Passing checks does not establish experimental authenticity.

## 06-overview — Inside the scientific workspace

Here is the actual application in demo mode. These deterministic simulated datasets cover catalytic kinetics, battery cycling, and a flagged submission. Watch the real workflow.

## 07-reject — Catch the inconsistency before exchange.

First, compile the flagged submission. Its reported Faradaic efficiency exceeds one hundred percent. The interface shows rejection, physical diagnostics, and a quarantined prompt injection warning.

## 08-attest — Inspect the evidence behind the record.

Now compile the eligible catalysis benchmark and inspect its attestation. Provenance connects the source laboratory, slice, check results, and priority record. Demo records remain labeled.

## 09-propose — Data for data.

Two laboratories offer complementary datasets. We propose a reciprocal exchange, then review exactly what each laboratory contributes and receives under the agreement.

## 10-first-pi — One approval is not enough.

The first investigator approves. Data remains locked. One laboratory cannot authorize both sides; a proposed collaboration is not yet an authorized exchange.

## 11-second-pi — Independent consent, on both sides.

The second investigator approves; the demo reports simulated settlement. In live use, the gateway verifies both local signatures before issuing reciprocal access grants.

## 12-catalysis — Catalysis, with units and conditions.

The catalysis view shows polarization and Tafel kinetics. This simulated benchmark reports forty two point five millivolts per decade, with units and experimental conditions visible.

## 13-battery — Battery behavior across cycles.

Battery cycling reveals capacity retention and differential capacity. Adjusting the range helps inspect changing features, without treating a curve as proof of a degradation mechanism.

## 14-context — Context is part of the evidence.

The causal warning matters. Alkaline catalysis and nonaqueous batteries have different conditions. Similar patterns do not establish shared causes; comparisons require calibration and context.

## 15-vault — Negative results. Positive research value.

The vault gives eligible negative results another route into circulation. Depositing failure data earns simulated SciCredit: internal data access credit, not cash.

## 16-market — Access is an explicit transaction.

In the marketplace, request access before receiving data. Review the payment challenge, complete the simulated purchase, then download the fixture. Authorization and delivery remain distinct.
