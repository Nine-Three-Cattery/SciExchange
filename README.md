## 🚀 Key Technological Pillars

### 1. The "Lean 4 for Science" Declarative Physical Compiler
- **Zero-Trust Ingestion**: Every data slice is compiled against an axiomatic constraint solver before listing.
- **Electrocatalysis Axioms ($\Phi_{\text{OER}}$)**: Enforces $FE \le 100\%$ (Faradaic Efficiency), thermodynamic overpotential boundaries ($E > 1.23\text{ V}$ vs RHE), and Tafel activation linearity ($R^2 \ge 0.98$).
- **Battery Axioms ($\Phi_{\text{Battery}}$)**: Enforces Coulombic efficiency bounds ($98\% \sim 100.5\%$) and polarization voltage cutoffs.
- **Instrument Noise vs. Scientific Negatives**: Validates $R_u$ solution resistance ($\ge 85\%$ iR-compensation) and OCV reference drift ($\le 5\text{ mV}$) to distinguish **high-value physical negative results** from human operator error.
- **Physlib-style Dimensional Typing**: Proves dimensional consistency across $[M^1 L^2 T^{-3} I^{-1}]$ (Voltage) and $[I^1 L^{-2}]$ (Current Density), preventing uncalibrated reference electrode anomalies.
- **Tensor-Ready Slicing (IUPAC/ASE)**: Normalized to $V_{\text{RHE}}$ and standard current density, directly consumable by PyTorch `DataLoader`.
- **Security & Prompt Defense**: Automated scanning against CWC Schedule 1/2 dual-use chemical registries and quarantine of indirect prompt injection vectors.

### 2. Proof of Priority & Anti-Scoop Protection
- Upon successful physical compilation, the platform mints an immutable cryptographic **Proof of Priority**.
- Guarantees timestamped authorship and attribution royalty before public release, eliminating the fear of getting scooped.

### 3. Bilateral Data Barter & Slice Liquidity Vault
- **Bilateral Barter**: Direct data-for-data swaps between complementary groups (e.g. Electrocatalysis OER $\leftrightarrow$ Battery Cycling).
- **Atomic Fair Exchange Escrow**: Solves the classic Fair Exchange Dilemma via Merkle pre-commitments and aggregated dual-key atomic release—neither party can unilaterally exploit or defect.
- **Data Liquidity Vault (Solving the Double Coincidence of Wants)**: Labs can deposit verified slices into the vault to mint SciCredits or settle via x402 USDC micropayments (powered by Cloudflare Virtual Wallets).

### 4. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Dual-Signing Governance
- Autonomous agents negotiate terms, align data schemas, and draft swap proposals.
- Final data release requires **Multi-Sig Dual-Signing (`Agent_Signature + Human_PI_Signature`)**, ensuring human scientists maintain ultimate decision-making control.

### 5. High-Fidelity Domain Analytics & Causal Guardrails
- **Catalysis View**: Computes LSV polarization profiles, $\eta_{10}$ overpotential (240 mV), and fitted Tafel slopes ($b = 42.5\text{ mV/dec}$).
- **Battery View**: Computes differential capacity ($dQ/dV$) degradation waterfall plots and bilinear knee-point battery cycle life estimation.
- **Causal Guardrail**: Actively alerts researchers to covariate shifts (e.g. 25°C vs 30°C temperature or alkaline vs acidic electrolyte mismatch) to prevent spurious causality.
